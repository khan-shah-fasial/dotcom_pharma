15. Final Status

Passed with Minor Issues

Recommendation

The discount setup screen saves, edits, lists, searches, switches off, and deletes the six discount kinds. The crashes and the confusing empty-save message found during this run are fixed.

Nothing critical is still open. One small display change (print the coupon number on the list) still needs one look on screen. The correct purchase-rate password was not tried again. No product in this data has two batches or a trade price above zero, so those paths were not proven with live numbers. Shop checkout does not use these discounts yet. That was outside this work, so this screen should not be treated as changing what a customer pays.

Retest the coupon number on the list. Do not deploy this as the customer price rule until a later pass connects it to the order.

16. Testing Limitations

  • Practice discounts were created on the live product Mouth Gag / DPI-M5 and then deleted. The older discount D-BW-001 was not deleted.
  • That product has one batch, code “-”, MRP 2310, and trade prices of 0. A second batch and a non-zero PTS or PTR could not be proven.
  • The correct password for the purchase rate was not submitted again.
  • Checkout, payment, public SEO, page-speed numbers, and other browsers were not tested.
  • Coupon change history keeps the latest 15 lines inside the discount row. There is no separate history table.
  • On a product or batch row, the list “amount” is quantity times rate. The discount amount itself is in the type column.
